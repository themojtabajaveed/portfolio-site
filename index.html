Projects

Scout - supplier and AI API research agent (private repo)

Opens supplier web pages in a browser, has Gemini extract prices and delivery terms, and shows each fact with a quote and source link. Facts it cannot find stay marked unknown. Runs through a local web interface. Checked on three UK supplier sites, and through the interface on one; 199 offline tests pass. A scoped prototype: matching delivery charges to order conditions is not reliable yet, and AI API price research is tested on sample data only.

DocsDue - UK accountancy AI agent (demo at docsdue.uk, made-up data)

Reads invoices, receipts and bank statements, flags missing documents and drafts a chase message for staff to review. It sends nothing. Gemini extraction with rule-based checks, an evaluation harness and a review queue, on a FastAPI and PostgreSQL backend with tenant isolation and an audit log. On a small fictional set, one prompt fix corrected mislabelled supplier invoices (3 of 3).
