01 / The flagship

Less chasing.
More accounting.

Missing-Docs Agent turns a folder of client documents into a checklist of what is still missing, then drafts the chase.

LLM extraction reads the documents. Plain Python rules compare them with the client's checklist. The dashboard shows complete, missing and unclear items alongside the email draft.

Human approval, day 3/7/14 reminder logic and a local approval audit trail are built into the prototype. Live sending and the database-backed workflow still need pilot testing.

03 / How we work

Your team makes the final call.

01

Review before sending

The prototype creates drafts and requires a named human to approve them. Approval writes a local outbox record; it does not send.

02

Keep the reasoning visible

Checklist results show the missing item, reason and rule source. The prototype also keeps a local record of draft creation and approval.

03

Agree data handling first

Every demo uses labelled synthetic data. Before a real-data pilot, we need to agree the purpose, access, retention and deletion rules, and the data-processing terms.

UK GDPR and ICO considerations are part of pilot preparation. This is not a claim of GDPR certification, ICO registration or readiness for real client documents. We will review hosting, AI providers and any international data transfers with your practice before a real-data pilot. UK-hours contact times will be agreed before it starts.
